FLORAF: A Differentiable FLORIS on GPUs

FLORAF is the FLORIS wake models and API with support for NVIDIA and Apple GPUs and automatic differentiation. This unlocks running large wind farm studies and gradient-based layout and yaw optimization in a fraction of the time.
