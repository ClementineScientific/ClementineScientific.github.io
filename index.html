About

Clementine Scientific is Rafael Mudafort.

I've started this business because I think the wind energy modeling tools produced at NREL and other research institutions are extremely powerful but yet inaccessible to much of the industry. I am bridging that gap by incorporating machine learning, differentiable physics, and agentic workflows along with performance optimization and workflow integration.

I've been a senior researcher in wind energy modeling at NREL for 9.5 years, where I coordinated the wind energy software portfolio, created the modern form of FLORIS from scratch, established much of OpenFAST's code quality and accessibility infrastructure, and enabled the coupling of many of NREL's models into other software. Prior to joining NREL, I worked in gas turbine computational fluid dynamics and general software development for web and high performance mobile applications.

My goal: Give wind turbine designers and wind farm developers faster, more capable modeling tools for the design of next generation wind energy systems.
